# Monday Priorities + Friday Ship — templates

*Two lightweight posts per person per week in #motion-by-design. Replaces "what's everyone on?" pings and makes blockers visible before they burn days.*

## Monday Priorities (per person, by 9:30am ACST)

**This week I'm shipping:**
1. [Job] — [milestone] — due [day]
2. …

**Blocked / waiting on:**
- [Job]: waiting on [who] for [what] since [date] ← blockers older than 2 business days get escalated by Dom/Yami, not re-waited

**Capacity flag:** [green / tight / overloaded — if overloaded, name what should move]

## Friday Ship (per person, by 4pm ACST)

**Shipped:** …
**Slipped + why + new date:** …
**Client waiting on us for:** … *(nothing should silently cross a weekend)*
