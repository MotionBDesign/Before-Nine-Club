# Project / Job Brief — template

*Copy this into the project channel (as a canvas or pinned message) before any design work starts. If a request arrives loose in Slack or email, whoever receives it fills this in and confirms it back to the requester. 5 minutes here saves a round of revisions later.*

---

**Job name:** `Client_Project_Deliverable` (this becomes the file naming prefix)

**Owner (MBD):** one name — who drives this to done
**Requested by:** client contact / internal person

## The change we're making (Hero & Guide)
- **Audience:** who actually sees/uses this?
- **Goal:** after seeing it, what should they think/feel/do?
- **Core message:** ONE sentence. If there are competing messages, rank them.

## The scene (Movie Time)
Where does this get used? (e.g. "Handed out at the Tactic Conference booth; reader has 30 seconds; must drive them to book a call.")

## Deliverable spec
- **Format(s):** e.g. A5 brochure, 16:9 deck, 1080×1080 social
- **Length/pages:**
- **Brand/assets:** links to fonts, logos, photos (NAS path + a mirror the whole team can open)
- **References:** what "good" looks like — link 1-3 examples

## Boundaries
- **Deadline:** date + time + timezone. If there's a hard external date (print, conference, tender close), state it and work back.
- **Revision rounds included:** (default: 2)
- **Budget/hours cap:** (if relevant)
- **Out of scope:** what we are explicitly NOT doing

## Checkpoints
- [ ] Concept/first look — date
- [ ] V1 for client — date
- [ ] Feedback consolidated by — date (owner: ___)
- [ ] Final artwork/handover — date

---
*Rule: no owner + no deadline = not briefed = not started.*
