# Client Meeting Recap — email template

*Send within 24 hours of every client call. The recap is the record — it prevents "that's not what we agreed" weeks later, and it's where scope changes get surfaced in writing.*

---

**Subject:** [Client] — [Project]: recap + next steps ([date])

Hi [name],

Thanks for the time today. Quick recap so we're working from the same page:

**Decisions**
- …
- …

**Actions**
| Who | What | By when |
|---|---|---|
| MBD (name) | | |
| [Client] (name) | | |

**Timeline check:** we're [on track / adjusted] for [milestone] on [date]. [If their action is on the critical path, say so: "We need the photos by Tue to hold the print date."]

**Scope notes:** [only if something discussed today is outside the current brief/quote — name it now, not at invoice time.]

Anything I've mis-captured, just reply and I'll update.

[sign-off]

---
*Rules: lead with decisions, not pleasantries beyond one line; every action has who + when; client-side dependencies are stated explicitly with their downstream effect.*
