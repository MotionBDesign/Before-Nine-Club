The Today
Board

A morning page, not a database. You open it, you find out what you have to do, you close it. Everything that is detail — burn rates, project histories, retainer maths — stays on Projects and Retainers where it belongs.

Two sections, because there are two jobs

Accounts is the client boundary — work arriving, work going out, work waiting on someone else. Production is inside the studio — what to make today. Yami and Andrea live in the first; everyone else lives in the second.

What your ClickUp actually holds

No, you will not be uploading screenshots

I went and looked at the real tasks rather than guessing. The answer to the thumbnail question is straightforward — but I found something more important while I was in there.

On the pictures

Every task I opened has zero attachments and an empty description. So thumbnails cannot come from ClickUp — but they were never going to come from you by hand either. Your artwork already lives in two places a machine can reach: the Drive folders you paste into Slack all day, and the studio share at MBD/homes/admin/Clients/…. Drive hands out thumbnails through its own API; the share needs a small watcher that grabs the newest file in a job folder. Either way it is a plumbing job, done once.

And the board never depends on it. Where no image exists you get the generated placeholder — client plus deliverable type — which is exactly what every thumbnail in these mockups already is.

The thing that actually matters

Most of your tasks have no assignee and no due date. Resmed's Tiered Bundles EDMs, the ATL + OOH ads, the Service days EDMs, the comorbidity artwork — all unassigned. Allocation is happening in Slack and never landing in ClickUp.

That flips the point of the dish-out screen below. It is not a nicer way to do something you already do — it is the thing that finally captures the allocation you are currently only saying out loud.

Three things I can now use exactly instead of approximately. A day is 6.5 hours in your own formula, not 8. Work is typed Retainer / Quoted / Non Billable / Sponsored. And you already have a health scale — On Track → Close to Short Time → Time for Review → Overtime — so the board should use those four words rather than inventing new ones. The screens below do.

What is deliberately missing

The things I left off

A morning page earns its keep by what it refuses to show. Each of these was tempting and each one belongs somewhere else.

Retainer burn and pace

Days delivered against promised, the pace race, the months view. It is the best thing on :3100 and it is a Retainers question, not a this-morning question.

Full project detail

Task trees, dependencies, file lists, history. A card here carries a title, a client, a size and one status. Clicking it opens Projects.

Everything under threshold

Clients waiting under two days, jobs due beyond this week, anything with no action. It exists, it is fine, it does not need a person this morning.

A test for anything you are tempted to add: would someone do something different today because they saw it? If not, it is a Projects or Retainers row. The Today tab is allowed to be the smallest page in the app.

Before this gets built

Four things I had to assume

All four change the build, and all four are yours to answer.

Does “ready to go off” exist as a status?

Your ClickUp has with the client and feedback alterations, but nothing that means “approved internally, not yet sent”. Column three needs one, or it has to be inferred.

What is the bump threshold?

I used 3 days to amber and 7 to red. It probably differs by client — Resmed answer in hours, Ilim in weeks.

Where does “new in” come from?

Right now briefs arrive as Slack messages in client channels. Something has to turn those into cards, or Yami keeps doing it by hand and the column is just a nicer inbox.

Do people get their own view, or one shared one?

I drew Production personalised to Kavindu. The alternative is one floor-wide board everyone reads. Personalised is better for the team, worse for Yami — she may want both.
